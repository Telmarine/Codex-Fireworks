# Codex: Fireworks changelog

## 1.0.1
- Launchers can be filled by hoppers and pipes. Each slot remembers its last rocket and is only refilled with that rocket. New Clear Slot Memory button.
- Firework Workbench copying: sneak-right-click with a rocket to set a template, feed it paper, gunpowder and firework stars, and pipe out the copies.
- Rocket copy crafting recipe: a rocket with paper, gunpowder and a firework star gives the rocket back plus a copy.
- Create: a Deployer holding a rocket copies it onto a plain firework rocket.
- CC: Tweaked: launchers, Show Controllers and Firework Workbenches are peripherals.
- New `[automation]` switches in `features.toml` and `[copying]` section in `limits.toml`.
