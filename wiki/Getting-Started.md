# Getting Started

## Items

| Item | Recipe | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Firework Workbench | Crafting table in the middle, gunpowder on the 4 sides, paper in the 4 corners | Building rockets with up to 8 bursts |
| Pattern Card | Paper + any dye (shapeless) | Drawing a picture or text that bursts in the sky |
| Launcher | Dispenser in the middle, gunpowder on top, 7 iron ingots around it | Firing rockets on redstone or from a show |
| Show Controller | Top row: redstone, clock, redstone. Bottom row: redstone, launcher, redstone | Running a timed show across linked launchers |
| Show Card | Paper + redstone (shapeless) | Copying a show from one controller to another |

All five are in the Codex: Fireworks creative tab.

## First rocket

1. Craft a Firework Workbench and place it.
2. Put in paper, 1-3 gunpowder and a few firework stars.
3. Pick a formation (Ring, Line, ...) and press Apply.
4. Press Preview to see it in the sky, then Craft.

## First show

1. Place a few Launchers and load rockets into them.
2. Place a Show Controller and shift-right-click it to start linking.
3. Shift-right-click each launcher, then shift-right-click the controller again.
4. Right-click the controller, press Add cue a few times, set the times and press Save.
5. Press Start, or power the controller with redstone.

See [Firework Workbench](Firework-Workbench) and [Show Controller](Show-Controller) for the details.
