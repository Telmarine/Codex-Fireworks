# Codex: Fireworks

A firework mod for NeoForge 1.21.1. It adds new burst shapes, rockets that pop into formations of up to 8 bursts, drawn patterns and text, launchers and timed shows.

Install it on both the server and the client.

Rockets made by this mod are normal vanilla firework rockets with extra data. They also carry plain vanilla stars, so if the mod is turned off or removed they still fly and pop like any other rocket.
