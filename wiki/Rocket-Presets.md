# Rocket Presets

Ready-made rockets. Give them with `/fireworks give rocket <id>` or make them in the [Firework Workbench](Firework-Workbench).

| Id | Name | Rarity | Flight | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `golden_willow` | Golden Willow | Rare | 3 | One big gold willow that fades to dark orange |
| `rainbow_ring` | Rainbow Ring | Rare | 2 | Seven rings stacked bottom to top, one color each |
| `crossette_storm` | Crossette Storm | Rare | 2 | A peony, then four crossettes around it |
| `heart_cluster` | Heart Cluster | Rare | 2 | Six small hearts around one big heart |
| `comet_fan` | Comet Fan | Common | 2 | Five comets in a fan |
| `strobe_field` | Strobe Field | Rare | 2 | A peony followed by blinking strobes scattered around it |
| `chrysanthemum_bloom` | Chrysanthemum Bloom | Rare | 2 | A chrysanthemum with late brocade glitter |
| `triple_break` | Triple Break | Rare | 3 | Peony, ring and crackle, each higher than the last |
| `spiral_tower` | Spiral Tower | Common | 2 | Four spirals stacked up |
| `fish_school` | Fish School | Common | 2 | Six fish bursts in a line |
| `palm_grove` | Palm Grove | Common | 3 | Three gold palms in a row |
| `grand_finale` | Grand Finale | Epic | 3 | All 8 slots in one quick rocket |

The rocket tooltip shows the flight, number of bursts, shapes and rarity.

Presets can be changed, hidden or added with a datapack. See [Custom Presets and Patterns](Custom-Presets-and-Patterns).

`rocket_presets = false` in `features.toml` turns presets off.
